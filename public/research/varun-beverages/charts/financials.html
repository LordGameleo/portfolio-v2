    My notes

      Source: Tapetide (Screener-derived consolidated financials) for Varun Beverages, twelve calendar years to December 2025, fetched 25 Aug 2026. VBL is a PepsiCo franchise bottler with a December year end, so every period here is a calendar year — “Dec 2025” is CY2025, not an Indian fiscal year. As reported: sales, expenses, operating profit and margin, other income, interest, depreciation, pre-tax and net profit, EPS and dividend payout (income statement); equity capital, reserves, borrowings, other liabilities, fixed assets, capital work in progress, investments, other assets and total assets (balance sheet); operating, investing and financing cash flow, net cash flow and free cash flow; ROCE, working-capital days, cash conversion cycle, debtor, inventory and payable days (reported ratios). Computed: net worth, capital employed, EBIT, ROE on year-end equity and on average equity (both plotted, because the year-end basis is punitive in the year of a large equity raise and overstates the CY2024 fall by 6.5 percentage points), net margin, the DuPont terms, capital expenditure, interest cover, cash conversion and the working-capital decomposition. Capital expenditure is derived as operating cash flow less free cash flow — no capex line is published, but free cash flow is defined as operating cash flow less capex, so this recovers the source's own capex figure exactly; it is cross-checked against the investing line, which exceeds it by ₹13cr in CY2025 and ₹94cr in CY2023. Reported ROCE is on an average capital-employed basis (reconstructed as EBIT ÷ average capital employed, matching within 1 percentage point in 9 of 11 years), so it is already smoothed across the equity raise while year-end ROE is not — the two are deliberately shown against different denominators and labelled. Borrowings are gross and on this data source may include leases counted as debt; they are not a leverage measure. Net debt cannot be established — no separate cash line is published, so the “net” series is borrowings less the ₹174cr investment book only and is an upper bound. Day counts are restated as a share of annual sales (divide-by-365) so they plot on a percentage axis; the day counts themselves are unchanged in the underlying series. The trailing-twelve-month income-statement column (through Jun 2026) is excluded from every series, total and ratio — it is not a fiscal year, and the balance sheet, cash flow and ratio tables were published in February 2026 and stop at Dec 2025, so any trailing-twelve-month ratio would mix a June numerator with a December denominator. CY2014 was a loss year, so the Indexed view is only meaningful for series that stay positive throughout. Every plotted value comes from one Tapetide dataset on one definition for all twelve years; other sources were used to cross-check and annotate only, never to replace a plotted value. Point-in-time data — not investment advice.
