Eicher Motors Ltd — financial dashboard, FY2014–FY2026

Consolidated. ⚠️ Two discontinuities in the earliest years, both from the company's own reporting rather than the business. Dec 2014 is on the PRE-DECONSOLIDATION basis — the commercial-vehicle joint venture was still consolidated line-by-line, so that year's ₹8,738cr of sales includes truck revenue that every later year excludes, and its 13% operating margin is not comparable with the 31% reported from Mar 2017 onward. The apparent fall in sales between those two points is an accounting change, not a decline. Mar 2016 is absent from the income statement — the year end moved from December to March, making it a 15-month transition period the source does not carry, so the sales, profit, margin, payout, return-on-equity and debt-to-sales lines BREAK there rather than being drawn across a year that has no figure. The intrinsic model uses a nine-year window from Mar 2018 for exactly these reasons. Every metric on its own panel — the series span ₹ crore and percentages across a 100× range, so one shared axis would be meaningless.
