    My notes

      Source: screener.in consolidated financials, fetched 20 September 2026. Every plotted value comes from one dataset on one consistent definition for all twelve years; the trailing twelve-month column is excluded because it is not a fiscal year. Three things on this company need stating before any panel is read. First, return on equity is plotted from FY24 only: shareholders' equity was negative in every year from FY15 to FY22, which makes the ratio undefined rather than merely poor — dividing a loss by negative equity produces a positive percentage — and the FY16 and FY23 profits came from debt-restructuring write-backs rather than from operations. The line breaks instead of asserting a continuity that does not exist. Second, reported net profit for FY25 and FY26 is larger than profit before tax, because the tax line is a credit from recognising past losses rather than a charge; it is not a measure of trading performance and it has already reverted to a normal rate in the June 2026 quarter. Third, the share count rose 2.73 times between FY17 and FY26 while sales rose 1.32 times, so every rupee series here overstates the per-share outcome. Borrowings are gross and include lease liabilities. External documents are used to cross-check and annotate only, never to replace a plotted value. Point-in-time data — not investment advice.
