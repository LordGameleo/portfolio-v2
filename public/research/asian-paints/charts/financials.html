Source: Tapetide (Screener-derived consolidated financials), fetched 22 Aug 2026. As reported: Sales, Net Profit, Dividend Payout % (P&L); Borrowings, Reserves (balance sheet); Cash from Operating/Investing/Financing Activity, Free Cash Flow (cash flow); ROCE (ratios). Computed: ROE (net profit ÷ year-end equity capital + reserves, not average equity), debt/sales, net margin, retained ₹. Dividend payout is dividend as a share of profit — not dividend yield, which is against price. Borrowings are gross, not net of the ₹7,062cr investment book. Every plotted value comes from one Tapetide dataset — one consistent basis across all 12 years, all derivations documented. External sources such as the Ind-Ra rationale are used to cross-check and annotate only; no panel value is ever swapped for a figure from another source, because a basis break mid-series is worse than either source alone.
